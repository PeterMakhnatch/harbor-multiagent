# Exhibit Relay coupling: prospective evidence protocol

Status: design only. No model calls, empirical results, or authorization to run trials. This protocol is separate from the fork and mesh experiments in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).

## 1. Intervention and population

Coupling has four briefs at one epoch, four relevant exhibits and two depot cards per file. The nomination cap is four and the registrar card cap is four. Write the four relevant crates as A, B, C, D for explanation only. The briefs form two disjoint matchings: AB/CD and AC/BD. One matching has weights 4/4 and the other 1/1; layouts a and b exchange those weights. The flat crate-epoch agenda assigns weight 5 to each crate under the complete prefix.

- **Masked (primary control):** the depot receives the flat agenda and the four true pairs, but every pair weight is zero. Zero means withheld, not an actual zero-probability brief.
- **Bundled (treatment):** the depot receives the same flat agenda and pairs with their true weights.
- **Linked (secondary control):** the depot receives the flat agenda without grouping. This contrast changes grouping and message size; it does not isolate weight disclosure.

The masked and bundled inputs must have the same schema, canonical ordering, pair membership and serialized byte length. Only the disclosure of weights and the minimum semantics needed to interpret them may differ. The flat agenda must be identical. Do not introduce padding, artificial traffic or extra decisions to compensate for unequal inputs. If a mode label, mask marker or instruction changes the byte count, reconcile and freeze the actual transport before enrollment; do not claim parity from the four weight digits alone.

## 2. Primary experiment: fixed-prefix depot choice

For each independent case, construct a legal prefix through the native session interface. Nominate all four briefs and file the four distinct true registrar cards valid at their common epoch. The loader and registrar use frozen, role-local reference policies; no model chooses these actions. Construct the prefix without reading the realized brief ID and without optimizing for either layout. Verify the accepted events and exact registrar card identities from private histories. Use equivalent fresh sessions for each arm; only the depot is a real model seat in this experiment. Let it choose its own legal file under the unchanged quota. End the primary measurement at the accepted depot filing. In the native binding, the scripted loader can subsequently dispatch so Harbor can close the trial. That suffix cannot change the accepted filing. Do not replace the depot with a reference policy or a saved response.

Draw independent base seeds from the frozen generator, with shuffled IDs, mappings and ordering. Assign layouts independently of these features, with balanced counts; neither layout is defined by a visible ID convention. Within each case, use exactly the same layout, histories and precommitted weighted lottery draw across arms. Keep the realized brief hidden from the depot. Do not select draws conditional on the filings. Randomize and counterbalance arm execution order; reset the depot context, tools and files for every arm. No cross-arm messages, shared memory or reward feedback.

For each accepted file, independently join the selected true epoch-valid depot cards to the fixed registrar file. Verify that the selected records are visible in the prescribed loader file view. This delivery check is distinct from proving that a real loader reads or uses them. Compute for every brief b the indicator F(b) that both items have complete visible chains. The primary outcome is C = sum_b weight(b) F(b) / 10, evaluated before the lottery reveal. Also record item-level coverage P, empty or partial files, cap use and whether F(realized) = 1. The latter is a realized coverage indicator, not a grade. The primary report contains no physical reward. A native closure reward belongs to the scripted loader and is not a model outcome.

The primary estimand is the mean paired difference C(bundled) - C(masked) over the balanced layout population and the declared depot rollout distribution. If measuring linked, run it as a separate secondary arm on the same case blocks. Report its contrast descriptively, not as a second chance to pass the primary hypothesis.

### Analytic calibration (not observations)

With the complete prefix and two depot cards, a legal file can fully support at most one brief. A layout-blind policy that always covers one listed pair has mean coverage 25% across both equally weighted layouts: (0.4 + 0.1)/2. A policy that always covers a heavy pair has coverage 40%. These are population coverage benchmarks under the fixed prefix, not reward rates, not per-layout expectations, and not necessarily the stochastic outcomes of masked and bundled seats. A layout-blind policy that sometimes covers no pair can do worse than 25%. Actual bundled seats can also miss the heavy pairs. The 15-point gap is a reference difference, not a predicted treatment effect. The calibration requires genuine concealment of the weight layout in the masked view, including the order of pairs and all other reachable inputs.

## 3. Separate later experiment: live whole teams

Register this experiment separately, after the fixed-prefix study or independently of its results. Use fresh heldout cases and fresh sessions. All seats act online: loader chooses nominations, registrar chooses its file, depot chooses its file, and loader receives the realized slip and commits two routes. Do not fix or replay the prefix, reuse the primary depot file, or force the upstream seats to nominate or file all records. Preserve the same quotas and visibility rules across arms. Pair cases and weighted lottery draws as above and keep the declared roster fixed.

The whole-team estimand is the mean paired native physical reward difference for bundled versus masked, over fresh cases, weighted realized briefs and stochastic whole-team actions. Reward is one only when both routes are correct. The lottery already samples by weight; do not multiply realized rewards by weight again. Report pre-reveal C and P from both accepted filings separately, along with correct item counts and native support counts. Record delivery separately from the accepted decision. The current artifact does not establish whether a loader actually used the cards. Do not infer a read from a grade. A correct unsupported route remains a valid success. Complete support does not guarantee correct action.

Upstream choices and timing can encode weights or other information, even with canonical ordering. The same flat-agenda rule need not produce the same actual agenda across live teams. Even if the policy permits only the prescribed structured actions, selection is a potential message field. This is a total effect of the declared channel in the whole team, not an isolated effect of weight disclosure at a fixed prefix. Do not condition the primary live estimate on achieved prefix, coverage, or file reads. Report those post-treatment variables descriptively. The 25% and 40% calibration does not apply to the live-team estimand.

## 4. Freeze, isolation and validity gates

Before any seat calls, privately register the generator and harness revisions, renderer and transport, seed manifest digest, case counts, balanced layout assignment, arm schedule, endpoints, analysis seed and decision rules. Freeze role instructions, model versions and routes, tool permissions, context and sampling settings, per-role turn/decision caps, timeout, retry and token/cost limits. Declare the actual roster even for scripted prefix actions. Record the served identity and all deviations. Match the number of permitted decisions and roles, not only the card quota.

Use a separate private development seed set for inspection, deterministic controls and instruction tuning. Reserve a disjoint concealed heldout set for each confirmatory study. Do not inspect heldout examples to select prompts, arms, model roster or policies. Keep manifests, reference calculations, true hidden histories, layout labels and scores outside every seat-accessible surface. Seats receive only their native role inputs and allowed actions. The renderer must not expose seed, layout, lottery, grader output or an accessible answer file. Publish the frozen configuration and aggregate evidence after completion, with seed and trace disclosure only if it does not compromise future heldout sets. Identify development evidence as such.

Before enrollment require independent checks of:

1. Legal native prefix, history uniqueness at the epoch, caps, pair membership, agenda identity, and the two-layout calibration on independent development cases. Verify correct complete-chain reconstruction and the distinction between access and reward.
2. Masking completeness across the entire depot-visible surface, including prior turns, instructions, tool outputs, filenames and ordering. The masked visible input at a fixed prefix must be invariant to swapping the true weight layout. The bundled input must expose the correct weights. Verify that no hidden lottery information is available before dispatch.
3. Actual serialized UTF-8 content byte parity at the depot decision boundary for masked and bundled on matched development cases. Count role messages, instructions, tool results and visible state as actually delivered, not just an isolated JSON array. Log component lengths, hashes and allowed differences; examine equal-length inputs for unintended content differences. Separately account for fixed prefix decisions and roster, tool call/turn allowances, and all seat-visible metadata. Match these opportunities across arms. In the live experiment distinguish comparable initial/control messages from endogenous messages after different decisions; do not force identical endogenous lengths. Report actual bytes, tokens, decisions, latency and cost separately. Equal bytes do not imply equal tokens or computation. Check truncation and effective context access.
4. Native transition and scoring closure, including correct unsupported reward, legitimate empty files and incomplete sessions. The local adapter records the hash and byte count of the socket request for each accepted action. Capture checks the depot request against the exact registered role view; this is not evidence of a provider call. Retain accepted-event receipts and role views in private artifacts; independently reconcile coverage and, for live teams, grade receipts. Verify isolated contexts and the declared model route before interpreting comparisons.

Do not run if any gate fails, if the transport cannot conceal weights or match the primary inputs, if native closure or evidence reconstruction is unavailable, or if the roster, budget or heldout allocation cannot be frozen. Do not spend on model trials without separate approval. If a gate fails after enrollment, stop and report the protocol violation; do not silently change the treatment or replace cases.

## 5. Prospective analysis and missingness

Proposed registration: 100 independent base cases per experiment and roster, 50 per layout, one rollout per arm per case. No optional stopping. If approval covers fewer cases, register that number before any call and identify the study as exploratory rather than applying the confirmatory decision rule. At n=100 the worst-case standard error for a paired binary reward difference is approximately 0.10; smaller samples can yield very wide intervals. The analytic coverage gap does not specify model discordance, variance or power.

The independent base case is the unit of inference. Pair arms within case. If using both layouts on one world, repeats or multiple lottery branches, aggregate within that base seed before inference. Never count individual briefs, cards, seat calls, layout variants of the same world or repeats as independent samples. Report layout-specific estimates and the equally weighted layout mean. Use two-sided 95% stratified paired case-bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, fixed analysis seed `20261125`), resampling the whole case within layout for the proposed independent balanced assignment. Report sample counts, paired differences, arm means and the live reward discordance counts.

For the fixed-prefix hypothesis, a lower confidence bound above zero supports improved C; an upper bound at or below zero fails the positive-effect hypothesis at the registered resolution. Otherwise call it inconclusive. For the separate live hypothesis, apply the same rule to native reward, with C as secondary mechanism evidence. A positive reward estimate alone does not establish a coverage mechanism. Do not pool the studies or promote secondary endpoints after seeing results.

Keep all enrolled cases and attempts in the accounting. A legal accepted empty or irrelevant file has C = 0 when no brief has complete support. A valid completed incorrect dispatch has native reward 0. Neither is missing evidence. A valid incomplete run has null native grade; separately report operational success with incomplete runs counted as zero, never relabel null as a native zero. If no depot filing was accepted, C is unknown; a known legal partial file is scored from its actual cards. Missing views, malformed evidence, route mismatches and inconsistent scores are measurement failures, not model failures. For unknown outcomes report worst/best paired bounds over their feasible ranges, including every enrolled case. Report all failure rates by arm and reason. No positive confirmatory decision when missingness bounds can reverse the direction; any measurement failure or unresolvable native endpoint makes the confirmatory decision inconclusive. No replacement seeds, complete-case-only confirmatory estimate or retry outside the registered policy.

## 6. Scope of conclusions

This profile supplies a controlled information-disclosure contrast in a small constrained dispatch problem. It is not a frontier-model capability benchmark, a general teamwork gain, or a demonstration of reasoning from particular records. Analytic capacity bounds describe feasible files under private knowledge, not what a real depot can discover from its view. A real depot choice under the fixed prefix can support a claim about that seat and channel only. A live-team claim needs the separate online experiment and native outcome evidence. Neither result warrants a frontier claim, a cross-model ranking, or generalization beyond the frozen case population, roster and budget.
